\corr{Matrix-Matrix Multiplication}{ 
Consider the following Matrix:
\[
	\begin{bmatrix}
		a & b \\
		c & d
	\end{bmatrix}
	\begin{bmatrix}
		e & f \\
		g & h
	\end{bmatrix}
	=
	\begin{bmatrix}
		ae + bg & af + bh \\
		ce + dg & cf + dh
	\end{bmatrix}
\]
}
