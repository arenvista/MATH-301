# Neighborhoods
> [!def] $\epsilon$-Neighborhoods
> In order to talk about distance between objects we use neigborhoods.
> Let $a \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\epsilon > 0$. Then the $\epsilon$-neigborhood of $a$ is the set:
> $$
> \begin{align}
> V_{\epsilon}(a): 
> & = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : |x-a| < \epsilon \} \\
> & = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : -\epsilon < x-a < \epsilon \} \\ 
> & = \{x \in \mathbb{R} : a-\epsilon < x < \epsilon+a \} \\ 
> & = (a-\epsilon, a+\epsilon)
> \end{align}
> $$
>  
> Note $(a-\epsilon, a+\epsilon)$ is the set of points that are $\epsilon$-close to $a$
> 
> > [!thm] $\epsilon$-Neighborhood Property
> > $$
> > \begin{align}
> > & \text{Let } a \in \mathbb{R}\\ 
> >  x \in V_{\epsilon}(a) &\land \forall ~\epsilon, \epsilon > 0 \\
> > &\implies x = a
> > \end{align}
> > $$
> >
> > > [!pf] Proof:
> > > $$
> > > \begin{align}
> > > \text{Suppose: }& x \in V_{\epsilon}(a) \land \forall ~\epsilon, \epsilon > 0 \\
> > > & \implies \forall ~\epsilon, |x-a| < \epsilon \\
> > > & \implies |x-a| = 0 \\
> > > & \implies x-a = 0 \\
> > > & \implies x = a \\
> > > & \end{align}
> > > $$
> 
> > [!hum] ELI5: The "Shrinking Bubble"
> > Imagine you are standing at a specific spot on the ground (we call this spot a).
> > - **The Neighborhood**: Imagine drawing a chalk circle around yourself. Anyone standing inside that circle is in your "neighborhood." The size of the circle is ϵ (epsilon).
> > - **The Property**: Now, imagine we shrink that circle. We make it smaller, and smaller, and tiny like a germ, and even smaller than that.
> > - **The Result**: If a friend (x) is still inside your circle no matter how incredibly tiny you make it, there is no room for them to be standing next to you. They must be standing exactly on the same spot as you. Therefore, x=a.
