## Cauchy Criterion
## Contractive Sequences 
A sequence is contractive if the distance between terms is fractionally decreasing
$$
\begin{gathered}
\exists~c \in \mathbb{R} ~s.t.~ \forall~ n \in \mathbb{N},\\
|x_{n+2} - x_{n+1}| \leq c |x_{n+1} - x_n| \\ 
\end{gathered}
$$ 

We can also validate this (most commonly) by hunting for this value $c$ as:
$$ \frac{|x_{n+2} - x_{n+1}|}{|x_{n+1} - x_{n}|} \leq c $$
