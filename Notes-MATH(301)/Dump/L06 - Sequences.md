# Sequences

*Original Note: [L06](../02_s/L06.md)*

## Definition and Examples
> [!def] Sequences of Real Numbers
> A sequence of real numbers is a function
> $$X:\mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}, \quad n \mapsto X(n) = x_n.$$
> Notations: $X$, $(x_n)$, or $\{x_n : n \in \mathbb{N}\}$.
>
> Examples:
> $$((-1)^n), \quad \left(\frac{1}{n}\right), \quad \left(\frac{1}{2n}\right).$$

### Recursive Sequences
Some sequences are defined recursively (by induction), e.g., the Fibonacci sequence:
$$x_1 = 1, \quad x_2 = 1, \quad x_{n+2} = x_{n+1} + x_n \quad (n \in \mathbb{N}).$$
