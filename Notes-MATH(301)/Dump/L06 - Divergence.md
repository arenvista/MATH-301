# Divergence

*Original Note: [L06](../02_s/L06.md)*

> [!def] Divergence
> A sequence $(x_n)$ diverges if it does not converge to any real number. Equivalently, $(x_n)$ does not converge to $x \in \mathbb{R}$ if there exists $\epsilon_0 > 0$ such that for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$ there exists $n \ge N$ with
> $$|x_n - x| \ge \epsilon_0.$$
> This is the negation of the definition of convergence to $x$.
