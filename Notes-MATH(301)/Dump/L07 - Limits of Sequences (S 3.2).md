# Limits of Sequences (S 3.2)

*Original Note: [L07](../02_s/L07.md)*

> [!def] Convergence of a Sequence
> A real sequence $(x_n)$ converges to $x \in \mathbb{R}$, written $x_n \to x$, if
> $$
> \forall\, \epsilon > 0,~ \exists\, N(\epsilon) \in \mathbb{N} \text{ such that } \forall\, n \geq N(\epsilon):~ |x_n - x| < \epsilon.
> $$
