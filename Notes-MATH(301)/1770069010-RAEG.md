# Section 2.2 - Absolute Value and the Real Number Line

> [!def] Absolute Value
> The absolute value, of $a \in \mathbb{R}$, is denoted by $|a|$. We define this as:
> $$
> |a| =
> \begin{cases}
> a  \quad \\
> 0 \quad \\
> -a \quad \\
> \end{cases}
> $$
>

> [!thm] Absolute Value Properties
> 1. $\forall ~a,b \in \mathbb{R}, |ab| = |a||b|$ 
> 2. $\forall ~a in \mathbb{R}, |a|^2 = a^2$
> 3. If $c \geq 0$, then $|a| \leq  |c|$ iff $-c \leq a \leq c$
> 4. $\forall ~a \in \mathbb{R}, -|a| \leq  a \leq |a|$ 
> 
[!pf]
