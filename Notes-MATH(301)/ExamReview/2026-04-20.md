[!def]
A sequence $(x_n)$ is Cauchy IFF:
$$ \forall~ \epsilon > 0, \exists~ n_\epsilon ~.~ \forall~ m,n \geq n_\epsilon, |x_m - x_n| < \epsilon $$ 

[!def]
A sequence is contractive if: 
$$ |x_{n+1} - x_{n+1}| \leq c | x_{n+1} - x_n | $$ 

A sequence is properly divergenet if: 
$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \pm \infty 
$$ 

Note $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{1}{x_n} = 0$ does not imply $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \pm \infty$
- Work via the $\alpha$ definition

Comparision of Divergence:
Let: $x_n < y_n$
- If $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = \infty \implies \lim_{n \to \infty} y_n = \infty$
- If $\lim_{n \to \infty} y_n = -\infty \implies \lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = -\infty$

Ratio Test:
- If $\lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{x_n}{y_n} = L$ s.t. $0<L$
