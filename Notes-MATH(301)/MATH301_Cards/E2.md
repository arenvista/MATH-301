### **The Sequential Criterion for Limits**

---

**Theorem Statement**
Let $A \subseteq \mathbb{R}$, let $f: A \to \mathbb{R}$ be a function, and let $c \in \mathbb{R}$ be a cluster point (or limit point) of $A$. Then the following two statements are logically equivalent:

1. **The Functional Limit:** $$\lim_{x \to c} f(x) = L$$
2. **The Sequential Limit:** For every sequence $(x_n)$ in $A$ such that $x_n \neq c$ for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $\lim_{n \to \infty} x_n = c$, the sequence of function values $(f(x_n))$ converges to $L$:
   $$\lim_{n \to \infty} f(x_n) = L$$
